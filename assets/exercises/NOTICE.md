# Exercise image notice

The start/finish exercise photographs in this directory are selected from
[yuhonas/free-exercise-db](https://github.com/yuhonas/free-exercise-db), an
open public-domain dataset released under the Unlicense. Filenames have been
normalized for FORGE and only the movements used by this app are included.

The images are illustrative. They are not a substitute for individualized
coaching or medical advice.
